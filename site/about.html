About Brian

BRIAN
BAYSFILM / EDITORIAL / POST

I’m a Southern California creative producer, editor, filmmaker, and post-production manager, now creating original films and creative content.

My background brings together an MFA in Film Production from Ohio University, a BA in Telecommunications and Film Studies from Purdue University, and more than 22 years in post-production at Deluxe Digital Studios.

As Post-Production Manager at Deluxe (2002–2025), I led global post-production teams, solved complex production challenges, and helped deliver entertainment for Disney, Paramount, Warner Bros., and Universal. I also developed tools and workflows that saved hundreds of hours across departments.

Today, I’m producing The Evil in Me, an original supernatural horror web series adapted from my feature screenplay. My work also includes Head Games, a promotional concept trailer combining cinematic editing, music, sound design, and AI-generated visuals, and The Test, an optioned feature screenplay and PAGE Awards Semi-Finalist. I bring traditional storytelling and new production workflows together, managing the creative process from screenplay through final edit and release.
