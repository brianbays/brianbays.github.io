About Brian

A storyteller.
A problem solver.

BRIAN
BAYSFILM / EDITORIAL / POST

I’m a Southern California filmmaker and editor who understands both the creative work and what it takes to deliver it.

My background brings together an MFA in Film Production from Ohio University, a BA in Telecommunications and Film Studies from Purdue University, and more than 22 years in post-production at Deluxe Digital Studios.

At Deluxe, I led global authoring teams, solved complex production challenges, and helped deliver entertainment for Disney, Paramount, Warner Bros., and Universal. I also developed tools and workflows that saved hundreds of hours across departments.

My creative work includes Head Games, The Evil in Me, and short-form editing. I’m drawn to the relationship between story, sound, and pacing—and to new ways of bringing an idea to the screen.

Outside the edit, I’m a guitarist and podcast enthusiast.
